# Privacy Policy (Draft)

> Legal Review Required: This draft must be reviewed and approved by legal counsel before production use.

## 1. Scope
This policy describes how merchant onboarding data is collected, used, and protected.

## 2. Data Collected
We collect profile data, business data, location data, submitted documents, and verification records.

## 3. Purpose of Processing
Data is used for identity verification, fraud prevention, merchant approval workflows, and platform operations.

## 4. Data Retention
Data is retained according to regulatory and operational requirements, including audit obligations.

## 5. Data Sharing
Data may be shared with authorized personnel and service providers under confidentiality obligations.

## 6. Security Measures
Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are applied to protect merchant information.

## 7. Your Rights
You may request correction of inaccurate account data through support channels.

## 8. Updates
This policy may be updated; continued use indicates acknowledgment of current policy versions.
