RAPEX Business Plan
Company Name: RAPEX Technologies OPC
Director: Irvin Jay Palacio Business Type: One Person Corporation Launch Target: January 1, 2026 Beta Testing: November to December 2025 Headquarters: Province of Cavite, Philippines
I. Executive Summary
RAPEX is an innovative mobile technology company that integrates ride-hailing, food delivery, shopping, and logistics services into a unified app experience. With a strong focus on empowering local businesses—including hardware stores, groceries, wet markets, auto/motorcycle supply shops, convenience stores (like 7-Eleven, Dali, Alfamart), and retail malls (e.g., Unitop, Divimall)—RAPEX offers hyper-localized service delivery to meet real community needs.
Our mission is to provide a reliable and convenient all-in-one platform that simplifies everyday transactions for Filipinos while supporting entrepreneurship and boosting regional commerce.
We are set to begin operations in Kawit, Imus, Dasmariñas, Bacoor, Gen. Trias, Tanza, Naic, Cavite City, Amadeo, and Tagaytay. Nationwide expansion will follow, progressing throughout CALABARZON, NCR, and other major cities across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.
II. Company Overview
RAPEX is designed to be the digital infrastructure of local commerce. Users will have access to various essential services in one place:
 Ride-hailing
 Food and goods delivery
 Shopping from local merchants
 On-demand logistics
Our platform helps small businesses increase their visibility, giving them an alternative to larger e-commerce competitors. RAPEX offers end-users options for delivery or in-store pickup, saving them time and increasing convenience.
Our app helps end-users save a lot of time with its efficient system. Users no longer need to fall in line; they can order items while engaged in other tasks. Once the product is prepared, they can proceed to pick it up instantly—eliminating long queues and making shopping faster and more convenient.
We operate on a professional-grade infrastructure maintained by a skilled IT team committed to ensuring platform efficiency, security, and high availability.
III. Vision and Mission
Vision To serve and connect customers with their favorite local businesses, empowering entrepreneurs and fostering community growth through innovative technology, exceptional service, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. We promote convenience, speed, and reliability while creating new opportunities for businesses and individuals alike.
Mission To become the central hub of digital life in the Philippines by offering a convenient, time-efficient, low-cost e-commerce and logistics platform. RAPEX will integrate ride-hailing, food delivery, shopping, logistics, and more—helping users manage daily needs and emergencies in one app. We invite local merchants and big industries to adopt the future of commerce with us.
